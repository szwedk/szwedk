THE PATH

Campus to the field.

  1. 2020

    Kellenberg Memorial High School

    Class of 2020, Long Island, New York — where the engineering itch started. Came back years later with a G1 humanoid in tow to show the next class what robotics looks like now.

  2. 2020–2024

    Sacred Heart University

    B.S. Computer Science, minors in Cybersecurity & Mathematics. Built IntelliPARK — a campus-wide smart parking platform running computer-vision cameras, ultrasonic occupancy sensors, and edge processing nodes.

  3. RoboStore

    Integration Specialist

    Joined the Unitree-RoboStore team — bridging engineering, software, and innovation for classrooms, labs, and industry. Designed a line of custom 3D-printed hardware for Unitree platforms.

  4. Now

    Head of Technology by day, everything else after hours

    Leading a robotics stack — humanoid commissioning, VR teleoperation, autonomous patrol. Nights and weekends go to websites, apps, weddings, and whatever else lands on the bench.
